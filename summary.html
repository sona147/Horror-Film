Movie Summary

In a village abandoned for decades, Adam returns to his old family home after his grandmother’s mysterious death. While exploring the house, he finds an old mirror covered with a black cloth in the basement. Once he removes the cloth, strange things begin to happen — he sees reflections of people who don’t exist, and memories that aren’t his. He soon discovers that this “Third Mirror” is linked to an ancient demonic ritual used to trap souls. Worse yet, the mirror has started to drain his own soul, tempting him with impossible promises. Now, he must choose: destroy the mirror and face the entity trapped within, or surrender to it and vanish forever.
