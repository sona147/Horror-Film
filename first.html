Welcome to the World of Fear

The film revolves around a cursed ancient mirror used to trap souls within it. Anyone who gazes into the mirror slowly begins to lose their identity, seeing memories that aren’t theirs, until they become completely absorbed into it. The protagonist learns that members of his family disappeared the same way, leading to a psychological and terrifying fight to save himself and uncover the truth behind the mirror before his soul is lost forever.
